My design process

Every product starts as a rough idea. Here is how I take it from the first sketch to a polished design that ships.

  1. 01

    Ideate

    Brainstorm, sketch and explore many directions quickly before committing to one.

  2. 02

    Research

    Talk to users, study competitors and understand the real problem and its limits.

  3. 03

    Define

    Turn findings into clear goals, user flows and priorities the whole team agrees on.

  4. 04

    Design

    Move from wireframes to polished UI in Figma, built on a consistent design system.

  5. 05

    Test

    Prototype it, put it in front of real people and fix whatever breaks.

  6. 06

    Deliver

    Final designs and a clean handoff to developers, then measure and keep improving.
